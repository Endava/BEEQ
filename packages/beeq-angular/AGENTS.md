# packages/beeq-angular

Angular wrapper for the BEEQ web components, as an NgModule (`src/`) and as standalone components (`standalone/`).

- The `directives/components.ts` proxies in `src/` and `standalone/src/` are generated by the `packages/beeq` Stencil build. Do not edit them; see [packages/beeq](../beeq/AGENTS.md).
- Hand-written code: `src/beeq.module.ts`, the `index.ts` entries, `src/test-setup.ts` and `scripts/patch-value-accessor-event-targets.ts`.
- Keep both the NgModule and the standalone entry working when you change either.
